We would announce that DangoPlayer support for Android 5.x Lollipop is nearing End of Life (EOL), which means that Android 5.x Lollipop would no longer be supported on future versions of DangoPlayer 

<!--more-->

## Why future versions of DangoPlayer would no longer be supported on Android 5.x Lollipop?

The reason of why support for Android 5.x Lollipop would be removed on future versions of DangoPlayer is because Google's AndroidX Media3 library (which powers DangoPlayer) would require at least API Level 23 (Android 6.0 Marshmallow) since version 1.9.0, as seen on <a href="https://github.com/androidx/media/releases/tag/1.9.0-alpha01">1.9.0-alpha01</a>, which means that support for Android 5.x Lollipop would be removed on future versions of DangoPlayer when migrating to AndroidX Media3 1.9.0

Also, Google ended support for Google Play Services on Android 5.x Lollipop on July 11, 2024

## What is the future of DangoPlayer, and how users of Android 5.x Lollipop devices will be affected?

The next update of DangoPlayer would be DangoPlayer 26.4 "Elio" Servicing Release 4, which is expected to update AndroidX Media3 to version <a href="https://github.com/androidx/media/releases/tag/1.9.0-rc01">1.9.0-rc01</a>, consequently dropping support for Android 5.x Lollipop, this makes DangoPlayer 26 "Elio" the last major version of DangoPlayer to support Android 5.x Lollipop (until 26.3 SR3 update)

The next major version of DangoPlayer would be DangoPlayer 27 "Unimarutchi", which is planned to be released on July 7, 2026, coinciding with the 3rd anniversary of the modern DangoPlayer app (initially known as DangoPlayer Uni), which was released on July 7, 2023

The way DangoPlayer users on Android 5.x Lollipop devices will be affected is that they will no longer receive new DangoPlayer updates beyond 26.3 "Elio" Servicing Release 3
